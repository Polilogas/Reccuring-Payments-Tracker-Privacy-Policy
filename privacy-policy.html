Recurring Payments Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information Recurring Payments Tracker handles, why it is handled, and which third-party services may receive information when you use particular features.

Information you enter

You may enter a profile name and monthly income, currency preferences, service and lender names, descriptions, payment amounts and frequencies, due dates, trial end dates, loan fees, and payment records. This information can reveal personal and financial details, so treat it as sensitive.

The app stores these records locally on your device. The developer does not operate an app account system or a server that receives or syncs your saved records. The app does not connect to your bank, initiate payments, or cancel subscriptions on your behalf.

Service logos

To show a matching logo, the app may send a service name or domain to Google’s favicon service. The request does not include your payment amount, income, payment history, or due dates. Google receives the network request and may process technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy terms. If you do not want a service name or domain sent for logo lookup, avoid using that feature or use a generic service name.

Advertising and consent

Free accounts may display clearly labelled ads served by Google Mobile Ads. Pro accounts do not display ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, and interaction information to deliver, measure, and protect ads, subject to your consent choices and Google’s terms and privacy policies. The app uses Google’s consent tools where required and provides available ad privacy choices in Settings.

The developer does not receive your saved payment records through the advertising SDK.

Pro purchases

Monthly Pro subscriptions and lifetime Pro purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment credentials and purchase processing. The app receives purchase and entitlement status so it can enable Pro features; the developer does not receive your full card or bank details through the app.

Backups and deletion

Pro users can export a JSON backup or import a previously exported backup. Export writes a file to a location you choose using Android’s document picker. The file can contain your profile, preferences, services, loans, and payment history. Anyone with access to that file may be able to read it, so store and share it carefully. Import replaces the corresponding saved app data with the selected backup.

You can remove locally stored app information using Clear all data in Settings, or by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Exported backup files are separate from app storage and must be deleted from their chosen location. Removing app data does not cancel a Google Play subscription; manage or cancel it through Google Play.

Data sharing and security

The app does not sell your personal or financial information. Apart from the specific service-logo, advertising, consent, and purchase functions described above, the app does not send your saved records to the developer. Network connections used by these features are made to the relevant third-party services over HTTPS. Google’s handling of information is governed by Google’s own policies.

Children

Recurring Payments Tracker is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children through an app account or developer-operated server.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same privacy-policy URL, with its updated date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Recurring Payments Tracker Google Play listing.

This policy applies specifically to the Recurring Payments Tracker Android app.
